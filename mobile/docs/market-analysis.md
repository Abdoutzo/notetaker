# MemoFlux market analysis

## What the market already covers well

1. `Otter` and `Fireflies` are very strong when the use case is a shared team meeting. They already offer transcripts, summaries, highlights, action items, and workspace-oriented collaboration.
2. `Granola` proves there is demand for a bot-free experience that feels lighter than classic meeting copilots, especially for knowledge workers on desktop.
3. `Notta` shows that templates and timestamps matter because users do not want the same summary shape for every conversation.
4. `PLAUD` shows that people value frictionless capture and answers that stay traceable to original audio.

## The gap worth attacking

Most products above optimize for one of these worlds:

- team meetings with shared workspaces and integrations
- desktop note-taking during calls
- dedicated recording hardware
- transcript archives that you process afterwards

The personal gap is different:

- private capture on the iPhone, without a meeting bot
- fast conversion from raw audio to an editable structured brief
- support for field visits, walk-and-think memos, client debriefs, and ad hoc reflections
- evidence-first reporting with timestamps and raw audio preserved
- local drafts first, backend AI second

## Product thesis for personal use

The winning personal app is not a generic recorder and not a collaborative meeting suite. It is a personal operating system for turning spoken context into reliable next actions.

That means:

- record or import in one tap
- choose a template before or after processing
- generate a report with `summary`, `actions`, `decisions`, `risks`, and `questions`
- keep the raw transcript and timestamps available as proof
- let the user edit before exporting

## Competitive positioning

| Product | Strong at | Weak for this idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Otter | live collaborative meeting notes | more workspace-oriented than personal |
| Fireflies | real-time notes and follow-up automation | strongest in shared meeting contexts |
| Granola | elegant bot-free desktop flow | less mobile and field oriented |
| Notta | templates and transcript-linked notes | still transcript-first rather than personal workflow-first |
| PLAUD | capture convenience and traceable answers | depends on hardware and broader subscription gravity |
